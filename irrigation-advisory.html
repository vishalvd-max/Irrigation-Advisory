AI-Based Irrigation Advisory System

The Smart Irrigation Advisory System uses Artificial Intelligence, sensor data, weather information and crop information to provide suitable irrigation recommendations.

The system supports efficient water usage and sustainable irrigation practices for sugarcane farming.

1. Important Irrigation Recommendations

Recommended Action:

Check soil moisture before starting irrigation.

Follow crop-specific irrigation recommendations instead of using a fixed irrigation schedule.

Avoid unnecessary irrigation when sufficient rainfall is expected.

2. Water Conservation Practices

Farmers should follow water conservation practices to reduce unnecessary water usage.

Use irrigation according to soil moisture, crop requirements and weather conditions.

Efficient irrigation helps conserve water and supports sustainable agriculture.

3. Agricultural Measurements

Water quantity may be measured in m3.

Smaller quantities may be represented in cm3.

Water is represented chemically as H2O.

Temperature can be represented as 25°C.

4. Agricultural Technologies

AI is used to analyse agricultural data.

IoT connects sensors and devices to collect farm information.

GIS helps analyse geographical information of farm plots.

GPS helps identify and locate agricultural plots.

ET represents water loss from soil and plants to the atmosphere.

5. Today's Advisory

Advisory:

Plan irrigation according to soil moisture, crop requirement and weather forecast.

Follow water-saving irrigation practices whenever suitable.

Advisory Note: Recommendations may change according to field conditions, sensor observations and weather forecasts.

Advisory Disclaimer

This advisory is intended to support irrigation decision-making. Farmers and agricultural experts should consider actual field conditions before taking irrigation-related decisions.
