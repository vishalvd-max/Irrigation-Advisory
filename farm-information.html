About Farm Information

The Smart Irrigation Advisory System collects information from farms, sensors, weather services and other agricultural sources to provide better irrigation decisions.

1. Irrigation Advisory Workflow

  1. Collect farm, soil, crop and weather data.
  2. Monitor soil moisture using sensors.
  3. Analyse the collected data using AI and ML models.
  4. Predict the irrigation requirement of the farm.
  5. Generate a plot-specific irrigation recommendation.
  6. Provide the irrigation advisory to the farmer.

2. Agricultural Data Sources

The system uses multiple sources of agricultural information:

  • Soil Moisture Sensors
  • Weather Forecast
  • Satellite Images
  • Crop Information
  • Soil Reports
  • IoT Devices

3. Stakeholders and Responsibilities

  • Farmer
    • Monitor irrigation recommendations.
    • Provide farm and crop information.
    • Follow suitable irrigation advisories.
  • Agricultural Officer
    • Monitor farmer and field conditions.
    • Provide agricultural guidance.
    • Validate important advisories when required.
  • Researcher
    • Analyse agricultural and sensor data.
    • Study crop and water requirements.
    • Support improvement of AI models.
  • Sugar Factory Management
    • Monitor sugarcane availability.
    • Use farm information for planning.
    • Support productivity and resource management.

4. Important Agricultural Technologies

AI
Artificial Intelligence is used to analyse agricultural data and provide intelligent irrigation recommendations.
IoT
Internet of Things connects sensors and devices to collect farm and environmental data.
GIS
Geographic Information System is used to manage and analyse geographical information of farm plots.
GPS
Global Positioning System helps identify and locate agricultural plots.
ET
Evapotranspiration represents the loss of water from soil and plants to the atmosphere.
